On a PHP version older than 8.3, this plugin now switches itself off instead of breaking your site, and administrators see a notice explaining why. That can happen when a host moves a site to an older PHP after the plugin was installed. On PHP 8.3 or later, nothing changes.

PHP 8.3 is supported until CWM Proclaim 11 is released. From then on, this plugin, Proclaim and the other CWM extensions require PHP 8.4 or later.

No action is needed after updating.
